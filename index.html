FAQ

What IT, security and procurement ask us first.

Something not covered here? A solution architect will answer it in the review.

Do we need to replace any of our existing systems?

No. Yardly sits between the systems you already run, whether that is SAP, Salesforce, Workday or a twenty-year-old IBM i, and connects through their existing interfaces. Nothing is ripped out. Most customers retire custom glue code over time instead.

How long until our first process is live?

Typically 8 to 12 weeks from kickoff to a first end-to-end process in production, including discovery, security review and a parallel run against your current setup. Every process after that is faster, because the connections already exist.

Where is our data stored and processed?

You choose. Run on Yardly Cloud in an EU or US region, in a dedicated single-tenant environment, or in a hybrid setup where an agent processes data inside your own network and only operational metadata leaves it.

What happens when a connected system goes down?

Transactions are queued durably and retried with backoff, so nothing is dropped. The process owner is alerted, and once the system recovers Yardly replays the queue in order. Every step stays visible in the trace.

Can our own engineers build and maintain processes?

Yes. Processes are built in a visual designer or as code in TypeScript or YAML, versioned in your own Git repository and promoted from development to test to production with approvals. Many customers start with us building and end with their own team in charge.

How is Yardly priced?

An annual platform subscription based on the number of connected systems and environments. There are no per-transaction or per-user fees. Implementation is a fixed fee per process, agreed after discovery.
