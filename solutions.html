The platform

One platform underneath every process.

The same four capabilities power every process you run on Yardly, so each new process is faster to deliver than the last.

01 · CONNECT

Reach every system

Maintained connectors for the systems large organisations run, and open standards for everything else.

  • 180+ connectors, including SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, Workday and ServiceNow
  • REST, SOAP, GraphQL, SFTP, EDI, JDBC and message queues
  • On-premise agent for systems behind your firewall
02 · ORCHESTRATE

Run processes reliably

Long-running processes with state, timers, approvals and compensation, defined visually or in code.

  • Visual designer, or TypeScript and YAML
  • Human approval and exception steps
  • Retries, idempotency and rollbacks built in
03 · GOVERN

Stay in control

Enterprise access control and change management, so integration stops being shadow IT.

  • SSO via SAML or OIDC, SCIM provisioning
  • Role-based access and an immutable audit log
  • Development, test and production with approvals
04 · OBSERVE

Know before users do

Every business transaction traced end to end, with health and SLAs per process rather than per server.

  • Search traces by order, invoice or employee ID
  • Alerts in Teams, Slack, PagerDuty or email
  • SLA dashboards for every process owner

How we work with you

From first conversation to first process live in about a quarter.

A fixed-scope, fixed-fee path to production, run jointly by your team and ours.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Discover

    We map your systems and processes with the people who run them, then pick the first process by value and risk.

  2. Weeks 3–5

  3. Weeks 6–11

  4. Ongoing

    Operate

    We run and monitor it with you under SLA, or hand it over to your own team with full documentation.
